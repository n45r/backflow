# Backflow: 1-minute video script

Timed to `runs/submission/backflow_v4_for_voiceover.mp4` (57 s). Read at a normal pace.

**0:00 – 0:06** (cameras, site plan)
Cities plan their drains with flood simulations. Checking them needs expensive sensors. But floods get filmed all the time.

**0:06 – 0:14** (3D site, drain fix)
Gemini 4 Argon read this test site's plan, built it in 3D, and even fixed a drain missing from its own scene.

**0:14 – 0:20** (Managed Agents)
Managed Agents test three theories of how the water gets in, each in its own sandbox.

**0:20 – 0:35** (the loop)
The simulation runs three versions. Each is drawn in 3D from the real camera's angle. Argon watches them beside the real flood and picks the closest. The settings move toward its pick. No sensors, just video.

**0:35 – 0:51** (the test)
Then the real test: a flood the model has never seen. Before, it was off by 34 millimetres. After watching one flood, it's off by 6. That's within the range of the researchers' own sensor-based tuning.

**0:51 – 0:57** (end)
Backflow. Flood video in, a checked flood model out.
